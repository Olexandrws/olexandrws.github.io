Privacy policy

Familiar AI Studio. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Who this app is for

Familiar AI Studio is a private tool used only by the studio's owner to manage the studio's own YouTube channels and video files. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google account data: the owner's YouTube channels (to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing time, and to read the channels' own statistics) and the owner's Google Drive (to store and fetch the studio's own video files).

What data the app collects

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person. It has no sign-up, no user accounts, no tracking and no advertising.

How data is stored

The access granted by the owner is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to anyone else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for the tasks named above.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access deletes nothing from the YouTube channels or from Google Drive.
