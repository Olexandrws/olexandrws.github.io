Familiar AI Studio

A private tool of a small animation studio.

What this app does

Familiar AI Studio is an internal tool. The studio's owner uses it to upload the studio's own short animated videos to the studio's own YouTube channels, to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing time, to read those channels' own statistics, and to move the studio's own video files through the owner's Google Drive.

Who uses it

Only the studio's owner. The app is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Google account access

The app asks the owner for access to the owner's own YouTube channels and Google Drive. It uses this access only for the tasks listed above.
